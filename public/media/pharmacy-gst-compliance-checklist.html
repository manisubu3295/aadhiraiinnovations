Pharmacy GST & Compliance Checklist

A working checklist for pharmacy owners to self-assess GST and operational compliance — whether you're running billing manually or evaluating software. Print this or save it as a PDF.

Billing & GST

  • Every invoice shows the correct HSN code for each medicine sold
  • GST rate (0%, 5%, or 12%) is applied correctly per medicine, not a flat rate
  • CGST + SGST is applied for intra-state sales, IGST for inter-state sales
  • Customer GSTIN is captured and validated on every B2B invoice
  • Invoice numbering is sequential with no gaps or duplicates

Audit Readiness

  • Complete invoice copies are retained and searchable for at least 6 years
  • HSN classification for each medicine is documented and consistent
  • Tax calculation breakdown is available per invoice, not just a total
  • Exemption documentation is on file for any 0%-rated sales

Inventory & Expiry

  • Stock is tracked at the batch level, with expiry date per batch
  • There's an alert or manual process to flag stock nearing expiry (30 days out)
  • Expired stock is physically segregated and never billed
  • Stock counts are reconciled against system/ledger records regularly

Operational Resilience

  • Billing can continue during an internet or power outage
  • There's a defined process for syncing/reconciling transactions after an outage
  • At least one other staff member can operate billing if the primary user is unavailable
